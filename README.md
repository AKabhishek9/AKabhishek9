# Research

### 📄 Published Paper — 1st Author
**"AI Algorithms for Predictive Maintenance of Smart Electric Drivetrain Components"**
*Journal of Scientific Innovation and Advanced Research (JSIAR), Vol. 1, Issue 1 — April 2025*

Reviewed and compared classical ML methods (SVM, Random Forest, k-NN) against deep learning architectures (CNN, LSTM) for fault detection and predictive maintenance of electric motors, inverters, gearboxes, and batteries. Co-authored with 3 CSE classmates at NIU.
